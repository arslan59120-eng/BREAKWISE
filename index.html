01 / INTRODUCTION

What is ABS?

ABS stands for Anti-lock Braking System. It is a safety technology designed to help prevent a vehicle's wheels from locking during hard braking.

ABS
ANTI-LOCK BRAKING SYSTEM

Helps maintain wheel rotation and supports steering control during hard braking.

01 Prevents wheel lock

Helps stop wheels from completely locking during hard braking.

02 Supports steering

Helps drivers maintain steering control while braking.

KEY IDEA ABS does not simply make brakes stronger. It helps regulate braking pressure to prevent wheel lock.
02 / THE BASICS

How Does Braking Work?

When you press the brake pedal, the braking system applies force to the wheels. Friction helps slow down the vehicle.

①
Brake Pedal

The driver presses the brake pedal.

→
②
Brake Pressure

Hydraulic pressure activates the braking mechanism.

→
③
Wheel Slows

Friction helps reduce wheel rotation and vehicle speed.

What happens when a wheel locks?

If a wheel stops rotating while the vehicle is still moving, it can lose some of its ability to respond to steering inputs.

WHY ABS MATTERS ABS helps reduce the chance of wheel lock by adjusting brake pressure automatically.
03 / INSIDE THE SYSTEM

Main ABS Components.

ABS uses several connected components. Each one has a specific job in monitoring and controlling the braking process.

01
◉

Wheel Speed Sensors

Monitor how fast each wheel is rotating and send information to the ABS control module.

02
▣

ABS Control Module

Processes wheel-speed information and determines when braking pressure needs adjustment.

03
⚙

Hydraulic Modulator

Controls hydraulic brake pressure based on instructions from the ABS control module.

04
◉

Brake Caliper & Rotor

Create friction at the wheel to help slow down the vehicle.

REMEMBER These components work together to monitor wheel rotation and regulate braking pressure.
04 / THE TECHNOLOGY

How Does ABS Work?

When the system detects that a wheel may lock, it can rapidly adjust brake pressure through three main stages.

01
↓

RELEASE

Brake pressure is reduced to help the wheel regain rotation.

02
Ⅱ

HOLD

Brake pressure is maintained while the system monitors the wheel.

03
↑

BUILD

Brake pressure is increased again when conditions allow.

A continuous process

These stages can repeat very quickly while ABS is active. The system keeps monitoring wheel behaviour and adjusting braking pressure as needed.

IMPORTANT The release, hold, and build stages help regulate braking pressure to reduce wheel lock during hard braking.
